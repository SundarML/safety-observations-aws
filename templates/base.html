📘 Safety Observation System – Help Guide
🔍 What is this application?

The Safety Observation System is a digital platform to record, track, rectify, and verify safety observations across locations. It replaces manual registers, emails, and follow-ups with a single transparent system.

🔄 Typical Workflow
  1. Observe – An observer reports a safety issue.
  2. Assign – The issue is assigned to an action owner.
  3. Rectify – Corrective action is performed with proof.
  4. Verify – Safety manager verifies and closes it.
  5. Track – Dashboard shows trends, overdue items, KPIs.
👥 User Roles
  • Observer – Reports safety observations.
  • Action Owner – Performs corrective actions.
  • Safety Manager – Verifies, closes, archives records.
  • Admin – Manages users, master data, system setup.
📊 Key Features
  • 📷 Photo evidence (Before & After)
  • 📅 Target dates & overdue highlighting
  • 📈 Dashboard with trends & KPIs
  • 📁 Archive & restore observations
  • 📤 CSV / Excel exports
  • 🔐 Role-based access control
🎯 Why use this application?
  • Improves safety visibility
  • Ensures accountability
  • Reduces incidents & risks
  • Provides audit-ready records
  • Eliminates manual follow-ups
🚀 Benefits to the Organization
  • ✔ Faster issue resolution
  • ✔ Data-driven safety decisions
  • ✔ Compliance & audit readiness
  • ✔ Strong safety culture
